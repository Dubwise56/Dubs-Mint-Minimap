# Dubs Mint Minimap

By Dubwise

Adds a minimap, with some options, doesn't hurt fps, ping tool for multiplayer.

Multiplayer ready

Click the minimap button in the bottom right control group, or set a keybind to open it.

## Minimap

- Draggable minimap window with adjustable size.
- Toggle it with the button in the bottom right control group, or with a keybind. The button can be hidden in the options.
- Shows terrain, water, buildings and optionally plants. Updates live as the map changes.
- Click or drag on the minimap to move the camera. A box shows the current camera view.
- Scroll wheel over the minimap zooms it in, and it follows the camera while zoomed.
- Click the anchor to lock the minimap view at its current zoom and position. While locked, clicking the minimap returns the camera to where it was when you anchored.
- Click the spyglass to highlight every thing on the map of the same type as your selection.
- Optional coordinates readout of the camera position.
- Works on any map size, including non-square maps.

## Markers

- Colonists, with a pulsing icon for downed colonists and a highlight for selected pawns.
- Prisoners, colony animals, wildlife and predators, each switchable.
- Hostiles and other factions' pawns, optionally coloured by faction.
- Fires.
- Small arms fire, mortar shells and other overhead projectiles, and drop pods.
- Attack alert pings when an enemy starts attacking something of yours.
- Respects fog of war, and Real Fog of War if installed. Hidden Anomaly creatures stay hidden until revealed.

## Customisation

- Pick the marker shape and colour for colonists, enemies, wildlife, prisoners, friendlies and colony animals.
- Scale markers and colonist markers separately.
- Set colours for ground, plants, buildings, water and fog, or switch to the real terrain, building and plant colours.

## Minimap modes

- Free: always available.
- Comms console: only works while you have a powered comms console.
- Rimatomics TACS: only works while a Rimatomics TACS radar is active.
- Shows a no signal screen with boot and shutdown sounds when the requirement isn't met.

## Multiplayer

- Multiplayer ready.
- Ping tool: mark a spot on the map with a keybind or a spare mouse button, and every player sees and hears it.
